About Deskling
Deskling

Little friends for your desktop.

Deskling is a tiny Mac app that lets a pixel friend wander along the bottom of your screen while you work, the way one is down there right now. When your coding agent needs you, they come and tell you. Go on, click straight through them.

Download for Mac Free. macOS 14 or later.
brew install --cask program247365/tap/deskling

Requires macOS 14 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel. Free and open source.

Read Me

I wanted Clawd to keep me company outside the terminal, so I made him a home on the desktop. Then he got friends. It's free, it's open source, and it's just me.

  • You pick who moves in. Clawd, Octo, Minx, Sol, or Luna, and you can switch any time from the menubar.
  • They make an entrance. Open the app and your pick drops in under a spotlight, lands, and gives you a wave.
  • They keep to the bottom of your screen, stopping to do their thing: Clawd fishes, Minx leaps onto a pumpkin, Sol sets and rises again. Flip a setting and they'll roam the whole thing.
  • They notice you. Bring the cursor close and they stop, turn, and look up at it.
  • They keep an eye on your coding agents. Connect Claude Code or Codex and they act out what the agent is doing, cheer when it finishes, and walk over to wave when it needs you.
  • Your agents can talk through them. deskling say "Tests pass" puts words in their bubble, from a script, a shell, or any agent that speaks MCP. If you've stepped away, they save the news and tell you when you're back.
  • They'll visit your lock screen, if you invite them. It's a trick macOS doesn't officially offer, so it's off until you ask. Confirmed on macOS 26 and 27; on older versions they may not find a way in.
  • They live in your menubar. No Dock icon, no windows.
  • They never take a click, a scroll, or a keystroke.
  • They step aside for fullscreen apps.
  • They come in Small, Medium, and Large. Try the menu up top.
  • They're light: about 20 MB of memory.

Mac only for now. Windows and Linux are planned.
